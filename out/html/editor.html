Make the game your own

Pick a card or an event on the left to change its text, what it costs and does, when it appears, and the choices it offers. You can also write brand-new cards and events, and playtest any of them straight away.

  • Your changes stay in this browser. The game uses them while Play with this mod is on.
  • Share a mod with Export; anyone can load it with Import.
  • Playtests never touch your saves. Add ?nomod to the game's address to play without the mod.

Make it part of the official game

Export your mod and send the .tamod.json file to the game's maintainer. They can apply it to the game's source with node tools/apply_mod.js your-mod.tamod.json, which writes your edited and new cards and events into source/scenes/.
